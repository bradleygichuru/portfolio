# Surface brief — Home / Portfolio OS

## Scope and visitor mode

Experience. The full-bleed desktop is the portfolio; the first view is the OS; the reader below the screen is the accessible, no-JS edition.

## Audience and job

Potential clients and employers, on desktop or phone. They should understand Bradley's range from the desktop, open at least one app or piece of work, and reach a real conversation. The OS must explain itself through a welcome window, a recognizable menu bar and dock, and visible ways into Work, About, and Contact.

## Chosen direction

**The Portfolio Desktop** — a full-bleed Portfolio OS inspired by the macOS desktop metaphor, with original naming. The user-supplied violet ASCII-hands artwork on near-black is the ground; the authored B lives in the welcome and chrome, not as a wallpaper glyph; a translucent light menu bar and dock; white/graphite windows with traffic lights (light windows by default, dark mode available); Search; Apps; Controls; and a real local Terminal. The apps are Work (a Finder-like project browser), Notes/About, Terminal/Skills, Mail/Contact, Web/projects, and About This Portfolio. No Apple logos or trademarks.

## Raises from the hand

- **Cyclorama → atmosphere states.** Light and dark are staged cues: the windows, menu bar, dock, and tone colors change together while the supplied artwork stays fixed; Controls brightness dims the whole screen.
- **User override → the supplied artwork.** The violet ASCII hands hold the frame and the welcome sits in their gap; the artwork stays still for legibility. The earlier generated B wallpaper and its pointer parallax are retired.
- **Darkroom → the Terminal as darkroom.** The Terminal is the one true-black app; skills emerge from commands rather than a static pills row.
- **Calendar → live time.** The menu bar date and clock are real; the OS feels current.
- **Wuxia → a held frame.** The first view is composed as one desktop, and the B is an authored mark, not a system glyph.
- **Accretion → the boot threshold.** The power-on screen is the one-way crossing from dark to the lit desktop.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a working full-bleed Portfolio OS desktop, not a webpage dressed as a laptop; the visitor uses the portfolio through the OS.

OWN-WORLD: A desktop on the user-supplied violet ASCII-hands artwork over near-black; the authored geometric B lives in the welcome, menu bar, app icon, README file, favicon, and sleep screen, never as a wallpaper glyph; a translucent light menu bar and dock; white/graphite windows with red/yellow/green traffic lights; the system UI stack; system blue selection; a mono Terminal. The Terminal is the one true-black app surface; the artwork is the ground, not a UI surface. No Apple logos, no glass cards, no hero-and-cards layout.

STORY: The visitor lands on the desktop. The welcome window sits in the gap between the artwork's two hands, names Bradley, gives the one-line thesis, and offers Open Work, Read About, and Say Hello. The dock and menu bar are the navigation; Work opens the project browser; a project opens the Web app; the Terminal answers `help`; Search finds anything; Mail opens the real contact channels. The reader below is the flat edition.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 100svh Portfolio OS desktop: the menu bar (B, Welcome, app menus, Search, Controls, Wi-Fi, battery, date/time), the supplied violet ASCII-hands artwork, the centered welcome window with the name/role/thesis and three actions, the desktop icons, the dock, and a tip notification. Open Work is the primary action; the dock provides every app.
